Culture Fit Decision Kit

This isn't who you want to become. Let's figure out what to do about it.

Reflection Tools

1. The Values Audit

Uncover why you feel uncomfortable and map the exact compromises you're making daily.

2. The Situation Decoder

Isolate whether the toxicity is company-wide, restricted to your team, or just your boss.

3. The Micro-Rebellion Plan

Reclaim agency without burning bridges through subtle, values-based boundary setting.

4. The Internal Transfer Plan

Navigate quiet moves to adjacent departments or safer pods inside the organization.

5. The Exit with Integrity

Plan a clean departure strategy while holding on to your respect and self-worth.
