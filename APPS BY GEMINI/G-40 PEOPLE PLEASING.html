People-Pleasing Reset

Stop disappearing into everyone else's needs. Reclaim your time, emotional bandwidth, and authentic voice without the crippling guilt.

1. Yes-Audit

Uncover what you say yes to, why you do it, and what it truly costs your well-being.

2. The No Script

Generate clear, direct, and kind ways to decline requests without long explanations.

3. Boundary Map

Identify resentments, map out clear limits, and plan enforceable action steps.

4. Self-Check

Decode your suppressed desires, reconnect with core values, and choose yourself.

5. Relationship Recalibration

Evaluate give/take ratios, audit energetic drains, and restore healthy reciprocity.

Tool 1: The Yes-Audit

"I say yes to everything and then realize I never wanted to."

Tool 2: The No Script

"I don't know how to say no without being rude or feeling overwhelming guilt."

Tool 3: Boundary Map

"I don't have clear limits because I've never let myself have them."

Tool 4: Self-Check

"I don't know what I actually want because I'm always focused on others."

Tool 5: Relationship Recalibration

"My relationships are built on me giving. I don't know which ones are real."
