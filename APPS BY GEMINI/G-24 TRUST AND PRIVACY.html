Trust & Privacy

Healthy boundaries or hidden secrets? Find the line together.

Privacy is the healthy space where individual identity lives. Secrecy is the intentional hiding of information that directly impacts your partner's wellbeing or safety. Use these tools to align expectations, decode anxieties, and navigate digital transparency.

Boundary Audit

Map expectations for phones, money, location, and social life to identify alignment and conflict zones.

Transparency Talk

Translate demands for openness or requests for privacy into core underlying emotional needs.

Phone & Account Protocol

Set clear, mutually agreed rules for digital devices and finances without resort to surveillance.

Suspicion Decoder

Differentiate between intuitive danger signals, past trauma responses, and unfounded anxiety.

Rebuild Plan

Construct a phased roadmap back to security after secrecy or betrayal has damaged trust.
