Reality Check: What Burnout Is Not
It is NOT laziness, personal weakness, or lack of discipline.
Burnout is a chronic physiological and psychological response to sustained workplace friction and unmanaged stress. A simple 3-day vacation won't fix structural overwork or toxic culture. Stop blaming yourself for failing to adapt to an unsustainable system.
