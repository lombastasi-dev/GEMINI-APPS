3 Questions To Clarify Urgency

Use these exact scripts when a new "urgent" task drops into your inbox:

"To ensure I align with your expectations: Should I drop [Current Task] to start this immediately, or finish [Current Task] by [Time] first?"
"What is the external hard deadline for this, and who is consuming the end output at that time?"
"If I can only complete a draft version by [Time], or a polished version by [Later Time], which fits the need better?"
