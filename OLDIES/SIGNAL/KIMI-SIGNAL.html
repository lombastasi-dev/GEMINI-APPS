First Date Coach

Conversation Starters

  • "What's something you're looking forward to this month?"
  • "If you could only eat one cuisine for the rest of your life, what would it be?"
  • "What's the best thing you've watched or read lately?"
  • "Do you have any hidden talents or hobbies most people don't know about?"
  • "What's your ideal way to spend a Saturday?"

Natural Follow-ups

  • "That sounds amazing — what drew you to that?"
  • "How did you get into that?"
  • "What's your favorite memory related to that?"
  • "Have you always been into that, or is it newer?"
  • "Who in your life got you interested in that?"

Things to Avoid

  • Don't interrogate — keep it conversational, not an interview.
  • Avoid talking about exes or past relationship trauma.
  • Don't check your phone repeatedly — give them your attention.
  • Steer clear of overly controversial topics early on.
  • Don't overshare personal problems on a first date.

Confidence Prep

  • Pick an outfit you feel great in.
  • Arrive a few minutes early to settle in.
  • Remember: they agreed to this date — they already like something about you.
  • Have a few stories ready about recent experiences.
  • It's okay to be nervous — most people are.

Starting Conversation

  • "I'm really glad we finally did this. How's your week been?"
  • "This place has great vibes. Have you been here before?"
  • "So, tell me something random about yourself."

Recovering from Silence

  • "I'm enjoying the quiet too — no pressure to fill every second."
  • "My brain just went blank for a second — what were we talking about?"
  • "Okay, random question time: [pick any starter from the Before tab]."

Showing Interest

  • "I love how passionate you are about that — it's really attractive."
  • "You have a great sense of humor. I wasn't expecting to laugh this much."
  • "I feel really comfortable talking to you. That's rare for me."

Ending Naturally

  • "I've had a really nice time. We should do this again."
  • "I'm going to head out, but I'd love to continue this conversation."
  • "This was fun. Let me know when you get home safe?"
  • "If you could have dinner with any three people, alive or dead, who would they be?"
  • "What's the most spontaneous thing you've ever done?"
  • "Beach vacation or mountain cabin?"
  • "What's a skill you've always wanted to learn?"
  • "Tell me about the best meal you've ever had."
