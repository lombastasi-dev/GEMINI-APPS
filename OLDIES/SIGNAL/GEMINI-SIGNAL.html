First Date Coach

Conversation Starters
  • "What's something exciting you've been working on or looking forward to lately?"
  • "If you had a completely free day with zero obligations, what would it look like?"
  • "What’s your favorite local hidden gem spot around here?"
  • "What was the highlight of your week so far?"
  • "Are you more of a spontaneous traveler or a meticulous planner?"
Natural Follow-ups
  • "How did you end up getting into that?"
  • "That’s interesting! What’s your favorite part about it?"
  • "Has that always been something you enjoyed?"
  • "What surprised you most when you first tried it?"
  • "Would you recommend that to someone giving it a shot for the first time?"
Things to Avoid
  • Deep ex-relationship debriefs or venting about past trauma.
  • Interviewing rapid-fire style without sharing your own stories.
  • Heavily controversial topics before establishing rapport.
  • Checking your phone frequently or placing it screen-up on table.
  • Monopolizing the conversation for more than 2 minutes at a time.
Confidence Prep Checklist
Starting Conversation
  • "Great to finally meet you in person! How was your trip over?"
  • "Love this venue spot—have you been here before?"
  • "How has your week been treating you so far?"
Recovering from Silence
  • "Random question, but what's the best meal you've had recently?"
  • "By the way, I was curious—how did you get into your current line of work?"
  • "So, what shows or music have you been obsessed with lately?"
Showing Interest Respectfully
  • "I really like your energy—it’s super refreshing."
  • "That’s impressive, I love how passionate you are about that."
  • "You have a great laugh!"
Ending Naturally
  • "I had a really nice time with you tonight. Let’s head out whenever you're ready."
  • "I should probably head out soon, but I really enjoyed chatting with you!"
  • "Thanks for a fun evening! Text me when you get home so I know you made it back safely."
5 Instant Conversation Rescues:
  1. "What's a hot take or opinion you hold that most people disagree with?"
  2. "If you won a trip anywhere tomorrow, where are you flying?"
  3. "What was your absolute worst job ever?"
  4. "What's your ultimate comfort food?"
  5. "Notice anything interesting in the room right now?"
