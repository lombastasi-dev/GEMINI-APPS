RESET
Reset provides relationship and self-reflection tools. It is not therapy, diagnosis, or professional mental-health advice.

You Deserve Immediate Support

It sounds like you are experiencing severe distress or feeling overwhelmed. Reset is a self-guided reflection tool and cannot provide immediate crisis support or therapy.

Please reach out to people who can help:

  • US / Canada: Call or text 988 to reach the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, available 24/7.
  • UK: Call 111 to reach NHS mental health services, or call 116 123 for Samaritans.
  • International: Contact your local emergency services or go to the nearest emergency room.
  • Trusted Person: Consider calling a close family member or trusted friend right now.
